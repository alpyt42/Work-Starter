# people/

One person page per person the owner works with, flat: `people/first-last.md` (lowercase, no accents), from [`kit/brain/templates/_PERSON-TEMPLATE.md`](../../kit/brain/templates/_PERSON-TEMPLATE.md). First **and** last name required: without a confirmed last name, cite the person in the mission and ask. Before creating a page, look for aliases and variants in the existing pages.

Professional content only (role, scope, expectations, way of working), each addition with its dated source. No private life, health, opinions or phone number. Pages of people who are no longer active go in [`archive/`](archive/README.md).
